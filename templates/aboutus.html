About TrollyNet

Revolutionizing online shopping in India with cutting-edge technology, unmatched customer service, and a passion for excellence.

Our Story

Founded in 2023, TrollyNet.in began with a simple yet ambitious vision: to create the most user-friendly, secure, and comprehensive online shopping platform in India. What started as a small team of passionate developers and e-commerce enthusiasts has grown into a trusted platform serving millions of customers across the country.

We recognized the challenges faced by Indian consumers in online shopping - from product authenticity concerns to delivery issues, from limited payment options to poor customer service. TrollyNet was born to address these pain points and create an ecosystem where shopping is not just convenient, but delightful.

Today, we partner with thousands of verified sellers, offer millions of products across hundreds of categories, and maintain the highest standards of quality and customer satisfaction. Our journey has just begun, and we're committed to continuously innovating and improving the online shopping experience for every Indian consumer.
